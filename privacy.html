Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

Welcome to Sparism. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit this website, scan our product catalog, or submit an enquiry.

Note: this policy covers Sparism's product catalog & enquiry website. If you purchase directly through our main online store, that transaction is also covered by our main store's own privacy policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to respond to your enquiry and, where an order follows, to arrange delivery:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, address, email address, and phone number, as submitted through our enquiry form.
  • Enquiry Details: Which product(s) you're interested in, and any message you choose to send us.
  • Technical Data: Our website hosting provider may automatically log basic, non-identifying technical information — such as IP address, browser type, and device type — to keep the site secure and functioning correctly.

2. How We Collect Data

We collect data through:

  • Direct interactions, when you submit an enquiry through this website.
  • Standard web server logs kept by our hosting provider for security purposes.

3. Payments

This website does not process payments. Submitting the enquiry form does not place an order or charge you anything — our team will contact you directly to confirm availability, pricing, and to arrange payment and delivery through Sparism's usual channels.

4. Data Usage

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and confirm product availability, pricing, and delivery options.
  • Contact you via phone, WhatsApp, or email about your enquiry.
  • Comply with Indian tax and legal obligations where applicable.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your information is only shared with:

  • Our Enquiry Storage: Submitted details are stored in a private Google Sheet, accessible only to the Sparism team, via Google's infrastructure.
  • Fulfilment: If your enquiry leads to an order, your name, phone and address will be shared with delivery/logistics partners solely to deliver your order.
  • Legal Compliance: Only if required by Indian law or government authorities.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking, marketing, or shopping-cart cookies. It does not currently set any cookies at all — there is no login, no cart, and no visitor profiling.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable industry-standard precautions to protect your data. Enquiry submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the Sparism team, protected by Google's account security. However, please remember that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Under Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your data (subject to legal record-keeping requirements for taxes/audits).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at +91 94878 87871.
